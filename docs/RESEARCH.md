# Car-parts product research

Research snapshot: September 2026. This is design inspiration, not a specification or visual copy.

## Patterns worth keeping

### RockAuto

Strong at rapid catalog/part-number navigation. The main search accepts vehicle/part concepts, and dedicated part-number search can narrow by manufacturer/group/type. For this project: keep part-number/product-code search prominent and add saved vehicles/fitment later.

Sources: https://www.rockauto.com/en/catalog/ and https://www.rockauto.com/en/partsearch/

### PartSouq

Strong OEM identification flow around VIN/frame or part number and diagrams. For this project: future vehicle/VIN module should help identify the exact part before procurement, especially for imported parts.

Sources: https://meta.partsouq.com/en/catalog/genuine and https://partsouq.com/en/how-to-place-an-order-on-the-partsouq-com-368.html

### FCP Euro

Customer account keeps order history and tracking numbers accessible after shipment. For this project: customer panel should combine requests, accepted quotations, orders and tracking in one timeline.

Source: https://help.fcpeuro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020952272-What-is-my-order-status-how-and-where-do-I-track-it

## Suggested future iterations (require approval before implementation)

- VIN/frame search and saved vehicles.
- Cross-reference/OEM part-number table instead of one real part number only.
- Product fitment (make/model/generation/engine/year).
- Supplier scorecard based on price accuracy, lead time and issue rate.
- Quote acceptance link with expiry and versioned terms.
- Customer notification preferences (SMS/WhatsApp/email).
- Shipment milestones and document attachments.
- Search analytics: queries with no results become sourcing leads.
- Product availability split into local stock vs supplier availability.
- Object storage for product/request images with virus/MIME validation.
